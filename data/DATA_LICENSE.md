# Data license

Curated circuit extracts and any files produced by `flywire-cortex fetch`
from FlyWire Codex / public connectivity archives are derived from the
FlyWire connectome (FAFB v783 and related public products).

FlyWire data is licensed under
[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
Derived circuit JSON in this project is likewise CC BY-NC 4.0: share and
adapt with attribution, non-commercial use only.
